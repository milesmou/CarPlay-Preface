# 星瑞车辆图标

资源：`common/src/main/res/raw/ic_xingrui.png`。
使用内置 imagegen 工具，模式为 edit，以原星瑞图标为参考重新绘制。
采用深蓝灰渐变背景，放大车辆主体，简化格栅、轮毂和反光细节，增强小尺寸显示时的轮廓与明暗对比。
设置页预览与 CarPlay 车辆返回按钮共用该资源，发送给 iPhone 时沿用现有图标尺寸限制。用户自选图标继续使用原配置。

编辑提示词：

> Edit target: the attached existing Geely Preface / Xingrui vehicle icon. Produce a refined square CarPlay vehicle-return icon that stays clearly readable when displayed at only 72x72 or 104x104 pixels. Keep the same recognizable silver-blue Geely Preface sedan, its vertical-slat grille and slim headlights. Rework the photographic image into a polished, bold automotive icon illustration with simplified broad surfaces, clean crisp contours, fewer larger grille slats and wheel spokes, strong light/dark separation, clear headlight shapes, and no tiny visual noise. Make the car substantially larger and more vertically prominent: use a nearly frontal three-quarter view with only a little of the right side visible, centered and occupying about 90% of the width and 75% of the height, all wheels and body inside the frame with small safe margins. Replace all of the white background and white floor with a full-bleed dark navy/slate blue background (#182A40), with a restrained soft lighter blue glow directly behind the car (#315A78) and a very subtle grounding shadow. Silver highlights on the car should contrast strongly against this background. The icon should feel premium and coherent with a dark blue automotive app, with no scenery, no text, no watermark, no border, and no rounded corners baked into the PNG because the application applies its own mask. Do not add busy reflections or photorealistic microdetail. Preserve the sedan identity and make the result look immediately legible and intentional as a small app icon.
